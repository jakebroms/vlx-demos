Hi Margaret,

Your VivaLynx membership is covered.

Oneida County Office for the Aging has enrolled you in New York State’s Aging-in-Place Pilot, and the pilot pays for your VivaLynx membership in full. There is nothing for you to pay, now or later.

VivaLynx keeps you connected to the people you choose. A short daily check-in lets your family know you’re doing fine, reminders keep your medications on track, and a video call with your daughter is one tap away.

Your access code NYSOFA Enter it once, when you set up your account. It’s the only thing you need.

Getting started takes about a minute:

  1. Get the VivaLynx app on the phone or tablet you already own. If you don’t have one, we can lend you a tablet with internet included.
  2. Create your account with your name, your email and a password. When the app asks for an access code, type NYSOFA.
  3. Invite your care circle. We’ve already invited your daughter Maria, since you named her at the hospital. Add anyone else you choose; each person decides whether to join.
