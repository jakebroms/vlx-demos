New York State Technology-Enabled Aging-in-Place Pilot

VivaLynx, covered for eligible older New Yorkers.

If you are 60 or older and your county Office for the Aging is part of the pilot, the State pays for your VivaLynx membership. A daily check-in, medication reminders and one-tap video keep you close to the people you choose, from your own home.

What you get to do

Live at home, with your people close by.

VivaLynx is an app for you and your care circle: the family, friends and helpers you choose. Everyone sees the same picture, and you decide who sees what.

Stay close to family, from anywhere

One tap starts a video call with a daughter in Albany or a grandson in Texas. When something needs attention, the people you choose get an alert.

Keep medications on track

A reminder at the right time, a tap to confirm, and a quiet word to your care circle if a dose is missed. No pill boxes to photograph.

Check in once a day

Ten seconds in the morning: how you slept, how you feel. Your family knows you are doing fine without calling to ask.

Reach a clinician without leaving home

Telemedicine is included. Talk to a clinician from your kitchen, and most visits end right there.

Give everyone one shared picture

Medications, appointments and care notes in one place, so your aide, your case manager and your family stop working from different lists.

Your care circle sees what it needs to see. You decide who that is.

VivaLynx meets HIPAA requirements. Your information is never sold, and every person in your circle has only the access you give them.
Who qualifies

Three things make you eligible.

You need all three. Medicaid is never required, and your insurance does not matter.

  • You are 60 or older and live in New York State.
  • Your county Office for the Aging is part of the pilot. The participating counties are listed below.
  • One of these is true: you are on a waiting list for EISEP or another county aging service; you or someone who cares for you receives caregiver-support services; or you are going home from a participating hospital.
Participating counties Erie, Monroe, Onondaga, Westchester, Saratoga, Otsego and St. Lawrence, with more joining through 2027. If your county is not listed, your Office for the Aging can tell you when it will be.

Not found

We could not match those details to a participating county’s list.

That happens more often than you would think, usually because the county has a different email on file. Here is what to do.

  • Call your county Office for the Aging and ask for the VivaLynx pilot. They can check their list, update your email, and enroll you on the spot.

  • Going home from the hospital? Ask your discharge planner to enroll you before you leave. They can set up your care circle with you.

Questions people ask

Before you check.

Is it really free?
Yes. The State funds the pilot, and it covers your VivaLynx membership for the pilot’s full length. There is no card to enter and nothing that starts charging later.
I do not have a smartphone or a tablet.
Tell your county office when you enroll. They may be able to help with a device and internet service for as long as you take part.
Can someone else set it up for me?
Yes. A family member or someone else can create your account, enter your code, and invite themselves to the care circle. Many people start that way.
What about my privacy?
VivaLynx meets HIPAA requirements. You choose who is in your care circle and what each person can see, and you can change that at any time. Your information is never sold.
Do I need Medicaid?
No. The pilot serves eligible older New Yorkers regardless of insurance.
What if I want to stop?
Taking part is voluntary. Tell your county office or delete your account, and that is that.
