New York State Aging-in-Place Pilot

VivaLynx, covered for eligible older New Yorkers.

If you’re 60 or older and your county Office for the Aging is in the pilot, your VivaLynx membership is paid for. A daily check-in, medication reminders and one-tap video keep you close to the people you choose.

Funded by New York State through the Office for the Aging · run by AgingNY · delivered by your county Office for the Aging.
What you get to do

Live at home, with your people close by.

Stay close to family, from anywhere

One tap starts a video call. When something needs attention, the people you choose get an alert.

Keep medications on track

A reminder at the right time, a tap to confirm, and a quiet word to your care circle if a dose is missed.

Check in once a day

Ten seconds each morning, so your family knows you’re doing fine without calling to ask.
Who qualifies

Three things.

  • You’re 60 or older and live in New York.
  • Your county Office for the Aging is in the pilot: Erie, Monroe, Onondaga, Westchester, Saratoga, Otsego or St. Lawrence, with more to come.
  • You’re on a county aging-services waiting list, receive caregiver-support services, or are going home from a participating hospital.

Medicaid is never required.

Questions

Before you check.

Is it really free?
Yes. The State funds the pilot, and it covers your membership for the pilot’s full length. No card, nothing that starts charging later.
Can someone else set it up for me?
Yes. A family member or someone else can create your account, enter your code, and invite themselves to the care circle. Many people start that way.
What about my privacy?
VivaLynx meets HIPAA requirements. You choose who is in your care circle and what each person sees, and you can change that at any time. Taking part is voluntary.
